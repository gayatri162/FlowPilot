FlowPilot — Problem Statement

People managing student projects, college events, or early-career tasks can find it difficult to turn a broad goal into a clear sequence of manageable actions. FlowPilot explores whether a simple, reviewable workflow builder can help users define success, identify constraints, break a goal into steps, and decide what to do first. The current prototype uses rule-based templates and is intended for early feedback; the user need and impact have not yet been validated.
